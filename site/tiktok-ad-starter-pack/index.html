What's included

  • Three distinct ad concepts for your product and audience
  • Three short-form scripts, timed for 15-second vertical video
  • Three copy-paste-ready video-generation prompts, one per concept
  • Caption and subtitle copy for each ad
  • Opening hooks and calls to action, with alternates
  • A production and editing checklist
  • An AI-generated-content disclosure template
  • Everything delivered as one ZIP file

Not included: finished or generated videos, paid generation credits, managing or posting to your accounts, and advertising spend.

How the pilot works

1. Enquire

Email us your business or product, your website if you have one, your target audience and a few lines about the ad you have in mind. Please don't send passwords, payment details or personal customer data.

2. Confirm the brief

If it's a fit, we confirm the scope and terms by email. You supply the product brief, any assets you want used, and the claims you're allowed to make. You confirm you have the right to use those assets and that the claims are accurate.

3. Receive your pack

Delivery is three business days after we have the complete brief, assets, approved claims and agreed payment, subject to capacity confirmed before you order. One consolidated revision round is included.

FAQ

Which products can you take on?

One legitimate product or service per pack, for one audience. During the pilot we don't take adult, political, gambling, medical or health-claim, financial or investment, or other regulated or high-risk advertising.

Will these ads go viral or sell more?

We can't promise views, sales, conversions or reach, and no one can guarantee a platform will accept every ad. The pack gives you well-structured, clearly disclosed creative to test.

Who is the presenter in your videos?

Giselle is a fictional, AI-generated presenter. She isn't a real person, a customer or a testimonial.

Do you use AI?

Yes, we use AI-assisted tools, and a person reviews everything before delivery. The disclosure template helps you label AI-generated content where platform rules require it.
